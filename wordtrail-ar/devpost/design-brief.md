# Design brief: WordTrail AR

> Proposal only. Not generated by the official learner-led `plan-lens` skill, not signed off, and not built with CLAD. Run that skill in the actual SPECS Base Template and let it create/approve its own brief.

## The one spatial moment
A learner looks at an arm's-length virtual table. Four large alphabet stones form a shallow arc, and one gentle pinch at a time builds a three-letter word. Completing CAT or SUN reveals a celebratory world-space object.

## Why it belongs on glasses
- Uses: draws in the world and leaves hands available.
- Better than a laptop: the phonics task stays next to physical workspace, without holding a phone or navigating a flat letter grid.

## Product or overlay
- Type: product (the virtual trail is the experience).
- What the real version would sense: no live camera recognition.
- Preview stand-in: a clearly virtual 70 x 45 cm tabletop placed in the room.

## Where things live
- Viewing: approximately 1 meter ahead, a little below eye level (default).
- Four sound stones: 10 cm across, spanning at most 55 cm on tabletop (default).
- Prompt: single 25 x 8 cm readable world-space card behind stones (default).
- Answer/reward: just above tabletop, only after success.

## Hands and input
- Sound stone selection: single SIK pinch to a large highlightable target.
- Next and Restart: large SPECS UI Kit buttons.
- Voice: none. Visible feedback is sufficient without audio.

## When things show
- Stone choices/prompt show at round start; progress slots update on correct selections.
- Wrong-letter feedback appears briefly by the stones without penalizing progress.
- Final model/label and Next show only on completion, then hide at next round.

## Done criteria
1. Table and stones remain world-anchored when the viewpoint moves.
2. Pinch C, A, T and see three progress changes then CAT reveal.
3. Wrong letter P gives gentle correction and does not advance.
4. Next advances to SUN; pinch S, U, N and see SUN reveal.
5. Restart restores CAT and empty progress.

## Out of scope
No live object recognition, voice, account, background recording, persistent anchors, multiplayer or more than two words.

## Risks and open questions
All dimensions and behaviors are proposed defaults pending learner decisions in the official skill. Real glasses comfort and actual SIK interaction feel are unproven.

## Build notes for CLAD
- Build for SPECS, runnable in Lens Studio Interactive Preview.
- Use SIK and UI Kit, not custom gesture detectors or hand-built pinch colliders.
- Use real scale and bright readable labels appropriate to additive displays.
- Build the working interaction first. Run LEAF scenarios for each criterion.
- Don't record, identify or follow people.

## After the build
Verify with LEAF, walk around via WASD and mouse in Interactive Preview, check readability and reach, make design-focused corrections, and record the ACTUAL Lens Studio preview for YouTube. Document what's verified and what is unproven.
