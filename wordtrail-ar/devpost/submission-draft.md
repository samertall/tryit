**Title:** WordTrail AR — Turn Your Surroundings Into a Learning Adventure

**Tagline:** Build words in your world with spatial sound stones and a simple pinch.

**About:** WordTrail AR is a proposed phonics learning experience for SPECS. Learners build CAT and SUN using four tabletop sound stones, progress feedback, forgiving retries and a visual word reveal. The experience prioritizes real-world placement and minimal overlays, rather than a floating screen.

**Audience:** Early learners with a parent or educator.

**Design:** A focused spatial moment at arm's length. Large world-space targets, restrained feedback, one input pattern, no vision or microphone dependencies.
